# Privacy Policy for Website Roast AI

**Last Updated:** May 28, 2026

Website Roast AI ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and handle your data.

## 1. Data Collection
We believe in absolute data minimization. The Extension **does not** collect, store, or transmit your personal data, browsing history, or keystrokes to our servers.

When you click "Start AI Roast," the Extension extracts the following data from the *currently active tab*:
- Page URL
- Page Title & Meta Description
- Headings and Button text
- A small sample of paragraph text

This data is sent securely via a temporary, one-time payload to **Pollinations AI** (our third-party AI provider) to generate the roast text and analytical feedback. 

## 2. Third-Party Services
- **Pollinations AI**: We use Pollinations API (`text.pollinations.ai`) to generate the roast content. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies. We do not send any user-identifiable information to them.
- **Razorpay**: If you choose to upgrade to the Premium Plan, payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We never see, touch, or store your credit card information.

## 3. Data Storage
Any gamification ranks, roast limits, and premium license keys are stored locally on your device using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your browser and is never uploaded to any remote database.

## 4. Permissions Used
- `activeTab`: Used strictly to read the text of the webpage you actively click "Roast" on.
- `scripting`: Used to highlight bad UX elements and inject visual effects (like the Eye Tracking heatmap) on the active page.
- `storage`: Used to save your gamification rank and premium license key locally.
- `tts`: Used for the optional AI Voice roast feature.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the GitHub repository or the Chrome Web Store support tab.
